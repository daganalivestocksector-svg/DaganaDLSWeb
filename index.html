Everything your group needs

Shared routes

Add a trail link or GPX file and everyone sees the distance, climb and estimated time.

Gear lists

Claim items like the stove or first-aid kit so nothing is packed twice or forgotten.

Meeting points

Pin the car park and start time. Members get a reminder the evening before.

How it works

  1. Create a trip. Pick a date and add a route.
  2. Invite your group. Share one link. No account needed to view.
  3. Hike. Open the trip page offline on the trail.

Questions

Is Trailnote free?

Yes during the beta. Paid plans for large groups may come later.

Does it work offline?

Trip pages you have opened can be read without a signal.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern phone or desktop browser.
